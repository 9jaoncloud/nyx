2. Mathematical Formalization of LIF Region Dynamics

The membrane potential \(V_i(t)\) of neuron \(i\) evolves according to the discrete leaky integrate-and-fire equation:

$$V_i(t + \Delta t) = V_{\text{rest}} + (V_i(t) - V_{\text{rest}}) e^{-\frac{\Delta t}{\tau_m}} + \sum_{j \in \text{Pre}(i)} W_{ji} \cdot S_j(t - d_{ji}) + I_i^{\text{ext}}(t)$$

where \(\tau_m\) is the membrane time constant, \(W_{ji}\) is the sparse synaptic conductance weight, \(d_{ji}\) is axonal delay, and \(S_j \in \{0, 1\}\) denotes action potential emission. When \(V_i(t) \ge V_{\text{th}}\), the neuron spikes and enters a hard refractory window \(\tau_{\text{ref}} = 2.0\,\text{ms}\).
