1. Introduction & The Systems Reliability Trilemma

Critical edge computing infrastructure—spanning hypersonic aerospace actuation, closed-loop neural prosthetics, high-frequency financial settlement, and air-gapped sovereign defense networks—requires mathematical guarantees of latency determinism and execution integrity.

Traditional runtime environments fail along three fundamental axes:

  • The Allocator-GC Jitter Bottleneck: Memory allocators introduce non-deterministic lock contention and fragmentation; garbage collectors introduce unbounded P99.9 latency spikes exceeding 15 ms.
  • Microarchitectural & Hardware Side-Channels: Software branch conditions depending on secret keys cause measurable cache-timing perturbations (> 120 ps), while commercial-off-the-shelf (COTS) silicon may harbor dormant silicon Trojans ("Ghost Paths") triggered by esoteric bus states.
  • The Post-Silicon Gap: Compilers designed exclusively for standard Von Neumann architectures cannot synthesize code for biological DNA storage matrices, photonic waveguides, or cryogenic superconducting logic.
