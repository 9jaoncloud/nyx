1. 3GPP Rel-18/19 Satellite NTN Direct-to-Cell (std.mobile.satellite_ntn)

Traditional smartphones lose connectivity whenever users travel beyond the 15-kilometer line of sight of terrestrial base stations. Nyx Planetary Horizons interfaces directly with spaceborne base stations (gNodeB / eNodeB payloads) across four distinct orbital regimes. Because Low Earth Orbit satellites cruise at extreme orbital velocities of 7.56 km/s (approx. 27,200 km/h or Mach 22), the high relative speed generates severe Doppler frequency shifts (up to ±40 kHz on a 2.0 GHz S-band carrier). Nyx autonomously calculates the terminal-to-satellite elevation angle and pre-compensates the transmit carrier before the radio pulse leaves the device, ensuring the spaceborne receiver detects the packet exactly on-channel.

2. Neuromorphic Event-Based Dynamic Vision (std.mobile.neuromorphic)

Conventional mobile cameras use synchronous CMOS image sensors that repeatedly record full rectangular grids of pixels at fixed frame intervals (such as 30 or 60 frames per second). This design consumes 450 mW to 800 mW of power, produces hundreds of megabytes of redundant data per second, and introduces severe motion blur at high angular speeds. In contrast, Nyx Neuromorphic Dynamic Vision Sensors (DVS) operate without frame buffers. Each autonomous pixel evaluates logarithmic luminance changes and emits an asynchronous spike event only when illumination exceeds a defined contrast threshold.

📐 Architecture Diagram 2: Neuromorphic Event Stream vs. Frame-Based Sensors

<8mW vs 450mW

Multimodal Neuromorphic Sensor Architectures

The Nyx neuromorphic subsystem is not limited to optical vision. It supports five specialized event-driven hardware modalities:

Modality 1

DVS (Dynamic Vision)

Pure Address-Event Representation emitting microsecond polarity spikes. Ideal for 24/7 hand tracking, AirPinch raycasting, and pupil saccade tracking at 4.85 mW.

Modality 2

ATIS (Time-Based Image)

Dual-photodiode pixel combining change event triggers with instantaneous local exposure time measurement, yielding absolute intensity without frame scanning.

Modality 3

DAVIS (Hybrid Vision)

Synchronous Active-Pixel Sensor (APS) frame grabber paired with an asynchronous event sensor on the same focal plane for hybrid neural networks.

Modality 4

Silicon Cochlea (Audio)

Spike-based bio-mimetic auditory filterbank for always-on voice activity detection, ultrasound gesture sensing, and acoustic keyword spotting at under 100 microwatts.

3. Zero-Knowledge Biometric Identity Attestation (std.mobile.zk_identity)

Traditional digital identity schemes force users to upload full identity documents (driver's licenses, passports, and facial photos) to remote cloud servers. This exposes millions of individuals to identity theft, tracking, and database breaches. The Nyx Zero-Knowledge Identity subsystem changes this paradigm: the mobile device's Hardware Secure Enclave acts as an isolated prover. The user's biometric template and encrypted identity credential remain locked in hardware memory. The Enclave synthesizes a cryptographic Zero-Knowledge Succinct Non-Interactive Argument of Knowledge (zk-SNARK) that proves compliance (such as "User Age ≥ 18" or "Authorized Doctor") without revealing the user's name, exact birth date, address, or biometric vector.
